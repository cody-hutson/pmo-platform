- (c) Where an item has no closing PR and no citing commit, count a delivering commit that the item's closure record names.
- (d) Count a criterion that fits no nature under the candidate it matches. An item whose majority is a candidate fits no nature.
- (e) A criterion that records a measurement of the existing state is a recorded baseline, not `investigation`, and is not classified.
