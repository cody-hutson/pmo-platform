Internal hardening of the release orchestrator (`release-hub` Mode R/O). Nothing changes in day-to-day platform use — this makes the platform's own release process safer, harder to leave half-finished, and clearer about the next step.

**What changed**
- The readiness pre-flight is strictly **recommend-only** — it can no longer change the very thing it assesses.
- It always **names the next step** (run the release, or clear this cleanup list first).
- A new **architecture-conformance check** catches methodology-hardcoding + risky rename cascades before they enter a release.
- The release driver **always claims a version and logs the deploy** — never "merged but not recorded."
- A **per-wave collision check** avoids two divergent builds of the same item.
