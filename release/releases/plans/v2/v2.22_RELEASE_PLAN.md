## Release Outcome Statement

This release lays the comms-and-facilitation reference substrate. It delivers: a K1 facilitation-techniques corpus seeded with its first delivery-lifecycle domain and surfaced by a real consumer (#56 foundation), canonical meeting agenda + recap output-format specs single-sourced out of `comms-writer` (#317), and a recap↔follow-up boundary contract that keeps a recap a reference to follow-up records rather than their sole container (#378). Outcome: facilitation techniques have a governed home a delivery skill can cite in-context, and the meeting-artifact format definitions live once, canonically, with a clean recap/follow-up separation.
