### Recommendations

**Merge/split — RECOMMEND: keep #144 and #413 SEPARATE (two issues, one release).**
Justification by dependency edges + capability coherence (not issue count): the two cards have **no dependency edge** between them and codify **distinct disciplines** (canonical-form-application meta-framework vs reconcile-vs-annotate edit behavior) — different load-bearing concepts, different completion conditions, different anti-pattern signatures. Merging them into one issue would conflate two independently-verifiable disciplines and muddy the per-issue AC greps. They legitimately share ONE file (`decision-discipline.md`) but that is a contention point to sequence, not a coherence argument for merging. Co-releasing them in **one release** (one branch, one PR) is correct — they are coherent as a *release* ("agent/artifact discipline codification"), capability-coherent under epic #1174, and the shared-file contention is cheaper to manage inside one PR than across two. **Two issues, one release, sequenced #144 → #413.**

**Release Class proposal — `novel`.**
- **Trigger evidence (per release-class-taxonomy.md § Class Enum):** `novel` trigger (a) fires — **≥1 issue introduces a new reference doc**: BOTH cards add a NEW `core/disciplines/*.md` doc (`canonical-form-discipline.md`, `reconcile-dont-annotate.md`). Trigger (c) also fires — ≥1 Stage 5 ADR-class design decision (the new-doc-vs-extend placement decision for #413; the shard-scoping + §2.1-wording decisions for #144).
- **Why not `routine`:** `routine` is disqualified — its trigger (c) requires "zero new files added", and this release adds two. Its trigger (d) requires "zero new D-class decisions", and Solutioning renders at least the #413 placement D-decision.
- **Why not `cross-cutting`:** `cross-cutting` trigger (a) needs ≥3 `pipeline/stage-*.md` files touched — #144 touches the *applicable* §6 shards, which Solutioning may well scope to ≥3, BUT trigger (b) (≥3 of the named governance surfaces) does NOT fire (only CLAUDE.md among the named set, and that is operator-deferred), and trigger (c) (≥3 in-bundle compositional edges) does NOT fire (only ONE shared-file edge). With the cross-cutting triggers borderline-at-most and the new-doc signal dominant, `novel` is the correct class. **If Stage 5 scopes the §6 shard edits to ≥3 stage files, the operator MAY re-classify to `cross-cutting` at Stage 5 per the Re-Classification Protocol (cheaper-to-stricter = CHEAP / HIGH)** — flagged so the decision is conscious, not silent.
