## Wave A engineering sub-scope (live-verified at branch HEAD)

**#1135 — canonical mode-heading separator standard.**
- Survey result: individual mode-declaration headings use two separator forms — **em-dash ` — ` (92 headings, majority)** vs **colon `: ` (33 headings across 5 skills, minority)**. No hyphen-minus, no en-dash variants.
- **Canonical form chosen:** `<H2-or-H3> Mode <ID> — <Name>` with separator ` — ` (space, em-dash U+2014, space). Rationale: clear majority (92 vs 33) + platform heading idiom. Reversibility: CHEAP.
- Documented in `canonical-skill-structure.md §4` with the exact checkable regex.

**#1136 — apply the separator.** 30 multi-mode skills total (≥2 individual mode headings). **5 need conversion** colon→em-dash: pmo-qa-auditor (9), change-management (7), delivery-engine (7), pmo-process-designer (5), pmo-technical-analyst (5). The other 25 already conform.

**#1132 — vestigial Mode-Detection.** Exactly 2 files: `operations/skills/project-initiator/SKILL.md` (`## Mode Detection`, redundant with `## Mode Selection`) + `core/skills/eval-writer/SKILL.md` (`## Mode detection`, redundant with `## Mode Selection`). NOTE: prompt-builder + intake-desk also carry a `## Mode [Dd]etection` heading but are OUT of #1132 scope (operator-locked to the 2 named files).

**#1133 — Guardrails (Platform) conformance.** Census at HEAD: **9 conform** · **27 bare** (26 plain-rename + pmo-qa-auditor special-case) · **12 missing** (insert) · **1 Extended** (pmo-qa-auditor, KEEP). Total non-conforming = 39.
