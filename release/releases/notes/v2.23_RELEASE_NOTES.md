# Agents resolve people from one maintained, never-committed people-graph

2026-06-26 · v2.23

PMO agents now resolve people — names, the owner of a project, who covers whom — from a single maintained people-graph instead of re-deriving them from each project's free-text notes. The graph is composed at read time from a roster you maintain plus the platform's existing people and resource records, the four leadership-owner fields become typed references to a person, and the actual roster never enters the repository.

## What changed for everyone

- **People resolved from one maintained graph.** When a skill needs a person's name, the owner of a project, or who covers whom, it now reads from one maintained people-graph rather than re-deriving names from each project's notes. *Why it matters:* names come out consistent instead of re-typed and re-misspelled per project, and the platform can answer who-does-what, who-covers-whom, and coverage-by-capability and route work to the right owner.
- **Leadership owners are real, typed references.** The project, portfolio, and program owner and the initiative sponsor are now structured references to a person, with a defined fallback for people outside the roster. *Why it matters:* an owner field resolves to one consistent person — with a clear place for an external owner — instead of an unparseable free-text string.
- **Your roster never enters the repository.** The actual people roster lives outside the codebase and is blocked from accidental commit; only a de-identified template ships. *Why it matters:* real names and contact details stay out of the shared code by construction, not by remembering to scrub them.

## Known limits

- The people-graph reads from a roster you maintain yourself. Until you fill it in from the shipped template, the people-aware features fall back to the prior per-project behavior — nothing breaks, but the graph has nothing to resolve against.
- The graph is for functional coordination — who does what and who covers whom — and is explicitly **not** an HR or performance system.
- Report issues at https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/issues with the `cluster: data-architecture` label.

## Reversibility

MODERATE / HIGH confidence. `git revert -m 1` of the integration PR reverses the whole release; the changes are additive — new reference docs and schema rules plus read-only skill edits — so reversion is clean before any reader binds the typed owner reference. Standard 30-day outcome window per the release class.

### Operator and engineering detail

**People roster — the data surface (#315)** — a closed-allow-list operator-instance roster (`people-roster.yaml`) with `{value, source, last_verified}` provenance and a `status` tombstone, shipping in-repo only as a de-identified template (`operations/templates/people-roster-template.yaml`). The filled instance lives out-of-tree and is never committed; an `extract-roster-needles.sh` step feeds the roster's names into the gitignored PII needle list so the pre-commit name scan can block a stray copy, and `.gitignore` is the second backstop. `project-entity-model.md` (§10/§8) operationalizes the Person and Resource entities by reference rather than by absorbing them.

**Capability/coverage graph (#1166)** — `people-coverage-graph.md` defines a read-time composed view over the roster plus the frozen Person and Resource entities (composed, not absorbed; nothing new is committed as a store) that answers who-does-what, who-covers-whom, and coverage-by-capability. It carries an always-on maintenance trigger set that auto-invokes the roster needle-extractor to close the staleness window, and a clarification queue for ambiguous resolutions.

**Leadership-owner reconciliation (#1898, ADR-040)** — the project / portfolio / program owner and initiative-sponsor fields are type-lifted from free text to a typed reference to a person with an external-person fallback. The decision is governed by founding **ADR-040** (mutual-exclusion between the typed and external forms plus a field-conditional resolution rule); the four new owner rules land in the one frozen-schema file (`entity-field-schemas.md`, single-owner), with the field type-lifts in `project-schema.md` and `project-entity-model.md` §4. The ADR took the next-free number at authoring after the design's draft number collided.

**Consumption (#1899)** — `comms-writer`, `tracker-manager`, `ppm-agent`, and `delivery-engine` each read names, owners, and coverage from the shared graph view, read-only; every skill edit ran through the `pmo-skill-editor` discipline, and three template-sync entries register the roster and clarification-queue templates against their actual readers. The four skills were materially edited but not version-bumped in the integration PR — their `version: v2.23` bump and `.skill` package rebuild land at release-cut via the Stage 13 version-bump chore PR.

**Governance + spike (#1900, #1897)** — `OPERATIONS.md` registers the graph, its ambient maintenance, and its consumption (artifact / owner / cadence); that card skipped Solutioning as a mechanical registration. The build was gated by the no-code spike (#1897), whose three accepted findings set the shape: operationalize Person by reference, a typed owner reference with an external fallback and a mandatory founding ADR, and a never-committed roster with a de-identified template.
