# Two checks that only warned now stop a pull request from merging

2026-11-05 · v5.01

Two checks that used to only warn now stop a pull request from merging when they find a problem, and both are required checks on the main line. The check that compares each workflow's declared gate settings with the live ones now says when it could not read something, instead of reporting a match.

> **Skip the rest** unless you open pull requests against the platform, run a release's dev testing or its close, or change a workflow's gate declaration.

## Who this affects

- Anyone who opens a pull request against the platform's main line
- Operators who run a release's dev testing or its close
- Anyone who edits the gate declaration at the top of a workflow file

## What you need to do

1. **Only if one of these two checks stops your pull request:** fix what the check names, then push again. Most often that is a stale hook-registry index, and the failure message gives the one command that regenerates it, or a skill count written into your added text that does not match the platform's roster. There is no setting to change.

## What changed for everyone using the platform

- **Two checks now stop a pull request from merging.** The pre-merge check set and the skill-count check used to report a problem and let the pull request merge anyway. Each now fails on a finding, and both are required checks on the main line, so a pull request they fail cannot merge. *Why it matters:* a stale hook-registry index or a wrong skill count can no longer reach the main line unnoticed.

- **The gate-rollout deadline check passes for the right reason.** It reads green because the rollout it tracks was decided and moved to enforcing, not because its deadline has not arrived. *Why it matters:* without that decision, the full platform check would have turned red on Friday 2026-11-27; it stays green because the decision was made.

- **The posture check says when it could not read something.** The check that compares what each workflow declares with what the main line actually requires now reports a check that claims to be held back with no recorded reason. It reads the required checks a ruleset delivers as well as the classic branch settings, and reports anything it cannot read as not evaluated rather than reconciled. *Why it matters:* a passing reconciliation now means both reads returned and agreed, not that something went unread.

- **Stage 7's required-check reads are counted at each close.** The three required-check readings a release's dev-testing pass records each have their own row in the platform's register of governance obligations, and the release close counts them. This release's close counted all three in 6 of 6 dev-testing outputs. *Why it matters:* whether dev testing recorded its required-check readings is measured, not assumed.

- **A malformed workflow gate declaration names its own workflow.** A workflow's gate declaration must now state whether the check is required or advisory, and a malformed one fails naming that workflow rather than another. *Why it matters:* whoever breaks a declaration is pointed at the right file.

## Known limits

- Two other checks declared as required stay held back on purpose: the behavioral-regression pass-rate gate and the cross-reference link check still report without blocking, and each has a recorded reason.
- If a ruleset on the main line ever adds a review, merge-queue, deployment, scan or workflow rule, every posture reconciliation reads not evaluated until the check learns to read that rule.
- The skill-count check does not see an added line whose text begins with two plus signs ([#8482](https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/issues/8482)).
- The dev-testing reference still defines a "settled" required-check count in two ways. This release's count used the reading the operator ruled on during the release, and making the reference say one thing is tracked in [#7525](https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/issues/7525).
- Nothing needs installing: no skill changed, and the rules mirror was redeployed when the release merged. The platform's recorded version moves to v5.01 when this close lands on the main line.

Report issues at https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/issues, and report a suspected vulnerability privately as the [Security Policy](https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes.

## Reversibility

MODERATE / HIGH. Undoing the whole release is ordered: un-register both required checks first, then revert the single release merge, then re-date the gate-rollout deadline past its escalation window. Each check, and the posture check's changes, can also be undone on its own, cheaply. The version tag is kept as the record that the version was claimed.

### Operator and engineering detail

**The two flips (#8325, #8424).** The pre-merge load-bearing subset is Checks 38 (hook-registry index freshness), 73 (bundle-metrics gate integrity), 77 (mirror-pair parity) and 78 (rules budget), run by `deploy.sh --check-required-subset`. It flips with its sentinel `.github/deploy-check-ci.enforce` and `GATE_ROLLOUT_PHASE` both reading `enforce`; the skill-count delta flips with `${WARN_MODE:-false}`. Both workflow headers declare bare `posture=required`, and `core/standards/gate-efficacy-standard.md` records each flip as REGISTER with its specificity reading and its owner. On `main` at this close, Check 74 printed `phase=enforce armed=2026-08-29 elapsed=68d` and GRADUATED to enforce. The operator registered both contexts in `main`'s branch protection at Stage 12, immediately before the merge; `main`'s live required set read 24 contexts, both included, after the merge and again at this close.

**The posture reconciler.** `reconcile-gate-posture.py` no longer lets a `warn-mode-initial` qualifier buy an exemption: a qualified declaration with no HOLD-family disposition row, absent from the live set, is the new *unrecorded hold* divergence. It reads `rules/branches/main` by allow-list beside classic branch protection. Of GitHub's 25 rule types, the 15 that restrict refs, commit content or file shape are ignored, `required_status_checks` is read, and the other 9, with any type added later, read degraded, so the run is NOT-EVALUATED rather than a verdict over an unread gate. The run on the merge-commit push read RECONCILED: 24 reconciled, 2 held (behavioral-regression and link-check), 0 unregistered, 0 unrecorded holds. Its self-test passes all 40 arms.

**A closed posture vocabulary.** The declarations ratchet `test_gate_efficacy_declarations.py` now requires the `posture` key and reads it as `required` or `advisory`, with an optional parenthesized qualifier, and it attributes every harness result to its own mutation, so a malformed header fails naming its own workflow. Two conforming headers, one bare and one held:

```text
# gate-efficacy: posture=required  enforcement=branch-protection:"Skill-count / IMP-XXX delta"  always-reports=yes
# gate-efficacy: posture=required(warn-mode-initial)  enforcement=branch-protection:"Behavioral-regression pass-rate gate"  always-reports=yes
```

**Stage 7's P1 to P3, measured at close.** The required-gate and mergeability reads in `stage-07-dev-testing.md` (P1 mergeability, P2 the settled required-row count, P3 the § 5.1 state) each gained a `**Runner:**` label and a gate-coverage register row with a Population. Stage 13 Phase A14 counted all three Populations, `stage7-required-gate-p1-reads`, `-p2-reads` and `-p3-reads`, at 6 of 6 over the release's six Stage 7 Dev Testing outputs; every Outcome limb is owned by #8325 or #8424. The P3 Population uses the count-stable reading of "settled" the operator ruled at Stage 7 (PA-013); stage-07's two wordings of it are routed to #7525.

**Routed, not filed.** The release filed no new issue. Its Stage 9 ledger owed none and folded four follow-ups into open owners; of the five recorded after Stage 9, three more folded and two fell below the filing threshold. The close-time line:

```text
proposed=0 · folded=7 · below-threshold=10 · reclassified=15 · late=5
```

Notes went to the owners: the sentinel and rollout-phase residual to #7021, the containment premise that reads false after this merge to #4714, the two meanings of "settled" to #7525, Check 71's retreat and `--strict` wording to #8549, the plan verifier's unescaped backslash to #6224, and three pipeline findings to #8106, #7011 and #8440. The release added no decision record, so there was nothing to ratify at this close.

**How it shipped.** Two cards on one branch, built serially in six steps, with two Stage 7 fix passes and a Stage 9 sync from `main` after the sibling release `releases-file-only-the-follow-ups-that-matter` merged first. Stage 8 accepted #8325 (2 of 2) and conditionally accepted #8424 (8 of 10), its one condition, the registration and its live read-back, discharged at Stage 12. The release merged at `804484fa` and claimed `v5.01` with a signed annotated tag: the branch carried the provisional `v4.82`, and the sibling's claim of `v5.00` landed first, so the next free minor was `v5.01`.
