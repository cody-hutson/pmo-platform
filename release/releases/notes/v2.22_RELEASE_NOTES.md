# One canonical meeting agenda and recap format, plus a facilitation-techniques library

2026-06-25 · v2.22

Meeting agendas and recaps now follow a single canonical format definition instead of one copied across several skills, a recap now points to tracked follow-up records rather than being the only place they live, and facilitation techniques (starting with estimation) get a reference library that a delivery skill can surface when it is relevant.

## What changed for everyone

- **One canonical meeting-agenda format.** When you ask for a meeting agenda, the result follows a single canonical format — the six required parts (subject, attendees with rationale, a one-sentence goal, numbered items with owners and time allocations, pre-read, and logistics). *Why it matters:* every agenda you get is complete and consistent, and the format is defined in exactly one place, so it cannot drift between skills.
- **One canonical meeting-recap format.** Recaps now follow a fixed order — Decisions, then Action Items, then Notes, then Key Roadblocks — with a `[RECAP]` subject line. *Why it matters:* the most decision-relevant content is always at the top, in the same place, every time.
- **Recaps point to follow-ups instead of holding them.** A recap now references discrete, trackable follow-up records rather than being the only container for them. *Why it matters:* an action item lives as a tracked record that can be followed to closure, instead of being buried in a one-off recap that no one revisits.
- **A facilitation-techniques reference library.** The platform now ships a governed library of facilitation techniques, seeded with its first area — estimation — and a delivery skill can suggest a relevant technique in context. *Why it matters:* practical "how to run this" guidance is now part of the platform instead of tribal knowledge, and it surfaces only when it is useful rather than interrupting you.

## Known limits

- The facilitation-techniques library ships with one area filled in (estimation). The other nine areas — retrospectives, planning, requirements facilitation, user-story writing, stand-ups, decision-making, discovery, prioritization, and conflict/alignment — are planned as separate follow-on additions and are not in this release.
- Report issues at https://github.com/cody-hutson/pmo-platform/issues with the `cluster: documentation` label.

### Operator and engineering detail

**Canonical meeting-artifact formats single-sourced (#317)** — two new K1 specs, `core/standards/meeting-agenda-format.md` and `core/standards/meeting-recap-format.md`, become the single source for those formats. `comms-writer` Types 2/3 and `references/channel-formats.md` are rewired to cite them by path with no inline copy remaining, and the `MTG-###` `Agenda` field in `tracker-manager/references/tracker-schemas.md` is reconciled to point at the canonical spec — so the agenda definition lives in two homes (the canonical spec plus that field's pointer), not three. `operations/templates/README.md` registers both specs as cited canonicals.

**Recap↔follow-up boundary contract (#378)** — the boundary clause is inserted into the recap spec, the Recap-vs-Follow-Ups rows are split in `artifact-generator/references/artifact-catalog.md`, and the `ppm-agent` emit path is rewired to produce discrete follow-up records. The canonical inventory edit lands on `core/standards/operational-artifacts.md` (the R9 path correction — operator-authorized at Stage 4; the comms-writer and ppm-agent runtime mirrors regenerate on deploy).

**Facilitation-techniques foundation (#56)** — a new K1 corpus at `core/standards/facilitation-techniques/` (README index plus the first delivery-lifecycle domain, Estimation, as a conforming nine-field card), registered in `core/specs/framework-catalog.md`, with a silent-by-default surfacing trigger wired into `delivery-engine` Mode D. The story was decomposed: only the corpus-foundation card shipped this release; the nine remaining activity domains are deferred to per-domain follow-on cards under Epic #1173, each independently shippable and each seeded together with its consuming skill surface.

**Skills and packages** — five skills were materially edited and bumped to `version: v2.22` (`comms-writer`, `ppm-agent`, `artifact-generator`, `tracker-manager`, `delivery-engine`); their `.skill` packages were rebuilt in-merge at release-cut. Version note: the milestone was recorded version-less at Stage 4 and settled on v2.22 (minor) at Stage 5 Collective Review, because the five-skill `version:`-bump surface left no valid version-less option.
