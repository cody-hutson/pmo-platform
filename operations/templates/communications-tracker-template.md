## Lifecycle Policy

**ACTIVE** — Open, awaiting response, or tied to an open RAID item / decision / meeting. Full detail retained. Reviewed every cycle.

**CORE** — Resolved, but drove a decision, closed a RAID item, or is part of an escalation chain. Full detail retained as evidence trail. Reviewed on demand. Rolls to Archive when the parent RAID/decision/cutover task is also closed AND 5+ business days have passed since resolution.

**ACTIVE → CORE** when: response received + no further action required + parent RAID/decision still open OR response changed a tracked artifact.

**CORE → ARCHIVE** when: parent RAID/decision/cutover item is closed AND 5+ business days since resolution.

**ARCHIVE** — Summary row only. WILL NOT SEND items move here immediately. Fully resolved items with no open parent move here after 3 business days.
